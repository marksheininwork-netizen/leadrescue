Help with your lead inbox.

Leads are saved in the browser and device where you created them. Use Export CSV before clearing site data or moving to another device.

Follow-up actions prepare a message for you to review. They never send anything automatically.

For help or early access, contact the project on GitHub. GitHub issues are public. Never post lead records, customer names or contact details, passwords, or confidential business information. Use a redacted or synthetic example and describe your device and problem.
