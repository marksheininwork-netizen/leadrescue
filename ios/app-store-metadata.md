# App Store Connect listing draft — LeadRescue

- App name: LeadRescue
- Subtitle: Follow up with every lead
- Bundle ID: `com.mariasheinin.leadrescue`
- Version: 1.0.0 (build 1)
- Category: Business
- Promotional text: Keep each inquiry, next step, and follow-up date in one tidy lead inbox.
- Description: LeadRescue is a lightweight lead inbox for independent service businesses. Record who reached out, what they need, where they came from, and when you plan to follow up. Search and filter by status or due date, prepare a response draft for your review, and export the list as CSV.

Your demo data is stored on this device in your browser storage. No lead is contacted automatically. This early-access version does not sync data between devices or provide a hosted business account.

- Review notes: Single-user demo. Add a lead, set a follow-up date/status, filter records, prepare a response draft, and export CSV. Data stays in browser storage; no message is sent automatically.
