Your leads stay yours.

LeadRescue stores lead records in this browser on this device. The app has no account, server, analytics, advertising, or payment processing.

CSV export creates a file on your device. Follow-up actions prepare a message for you to review; they never send anything automatically. Requesting early access opens GitHub and shares only what you choose to submit there.

Clearing this browser’s site data removes locally saved leads. Export a CSV first if you want a backup.

Questions: use the Request early access link in the app and include only information you choose to share.
